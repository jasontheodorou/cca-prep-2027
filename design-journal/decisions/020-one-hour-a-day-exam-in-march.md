# 020 One hour a day, exam in March

## Context

Jason, 26 November 2026: "this is not going well, at all. I need to commit to this. It needs to be one hour of work a day on this, with the goal of ultimately taking and passing the exam by march 2027. I am dragging my heels on this but its totally achievable." Only one session had been done since the 10 November plan started.

Then: "start the calendar on the momentum screen from december 2026 please, and have it only go up to end of march 2027. thats how long I am giving myself to learn and apply all of this. lets assume the next 4 days are just me getting my head around core concepts. start the study from december".

## Options considered

- Give each lesson two full days. Rejected: it needs about 97 days and only 81 are free before the exam, even with no spare days.
- Drop the Friday spare day to gain 13 days. Rejected: a plan with no slack fails at the first missed day, and many days have been missed.
- Keep the same work and put two half-hour jobs in each day. Chosen: it fits exactly and keeps Fridays spare.

## Decision

- Exam: Saturday 27 March 2027, the last Saturday in March. Not booked. Booking is an optional 15-minute job on Friday 4 December.
- 1 December to 25 March: 81 study days of one hour (81 hours), 67 with hands-on work, 14 study weeks. Christmas (21 December to 3 January) and the February holiday (8 to 14 February) keep their dates.
- A lesson still has three half-hour jobs: read, build, test. Two jobs fit in a day, so two lessons take three days.
- Each section ends with one day of 20 questions and fixing the worst two, then its exercise at two steps a day.
- Practice exams are two one-hour sittings of 30 questions each.
- Library days stay optional: 15 January, 29 January, 5 March and 19 March.
- The plan file starts on 1 December. Days before it are no longer in the schedule, and the calendar shows December to March only. The study log keeps its old entries.
- A two-job day lists its jobs in `parts`. `build-how-to.py` joins the step-by-step help for both jobs under the day's title.

## What was cut to fit

- "Write four questions of your own" for Agents. The other four sections keep it, on the last day of their exercise.
- The test of all five sections, "Practice questions: every section" and "Explain it out loud" in the second look. The two practice exams cover the same ground.
- Lesson 2.5 builds and tests in one half hour.
- The "Welcome back" day after each holiday.
- Each exam situation is read once in the second look, with situations 3 and 5 sharing a day.

## Consequences

- The plan has no room beyond the Friday spare days: 8 plain spare Fridays, plus 4 library Fridays that can be used to catch up.
- About one hour of hands-on work per lesson, as before. The pace doubles; the amount of work does not.
- Without library day 4, the real exam is the first 2-hour sitting.
- There is no planned second try.
- Old plan kept at `artefacts/2026-11-26-schedule-26-june-exam.json`, with its script and help file beside it.

- Replaced later on 26 November 2026 by decision 021: 100 study days in four phases, exam on 24 April.
