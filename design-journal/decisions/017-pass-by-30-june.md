# 017 Pass by 30 June: one exam on 26 June, with the four exercises

## Context

Jason, 10 November 2026, after asking for and getting a guess at his chances: "OK 30 minutes per day is good. Lets give until June 30 2027 to pass the exam, that is exactly six months and 30 minutes per day. Update the plan please". The 9 November session had not been done, so the plan starts on 10 November.

## Options considered

- Keep the first try on 29 May and add a second try in late June. Rejected: three weeks is little time to repair, and the rules for taking the exam again are not known.
- One exam on Saturday 26 June, with the extra weeks spent on hands-on work. Chosen: more practice was Jason's main complaint about the old plan.
- Keep the August second try as well. Rejected: it falls after the deadline he set.

## Decision

- Exam: Saturday 26 June 2027, the last Saturday before 30 June. Not booked.
- 10 November to 24 June: 171 sessions (86 hours), 94 hands-on, 29 study weeks.
- New: each section ends with one of the exam guide's four exercises, one step a day, built in an empty folder. Exercise 2 follows Claude Code, 3 follows Prompts, 1 follows Tools and MCP, 4 follows Context. Each step has a tick on the Progress page.
- Library days stay optional and move: 29 January, 12 March and 7 May finish and repeat the exercises; 28 May is a full 2-hour practice exam.
- The second-try plan (7 June to 28 August) is removed. A copy of the script that made it is at `artefacts/2026-11-10-build-plan-two-tries.py.txt`.

## Consequences

- There is no planned second try. If the first is not a pass, the plan needs a new decision.
- About 1 hour of hands-on work per lesson plus about 2.5 hours per exercise.
- No holiday is marked between February and the exam.
